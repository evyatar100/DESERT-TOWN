### Sensitive: what the game must NOT trivialize
- Real grief. Never score, rank or "win" mourning, and never make the burn a loot or reward moment.
- No fail state or "game over" that touches the honorees' memory. Repeating an attempt is fine; a failure joke about the dead is not.
- Offerings stay private. Do not show a "camera" or screenshot mechanic on offerings (this mirrors the Guardian rule).
- Do not have the player climb or vandalize the Temple, or bring fire inside before the burn.
- The Temple burn is silent. Never put party music or fireworks on the Temple burn; the party belongs to the Man burn.
- The four honorees died 2–3 months ago. Statues and dialogue must not mock them, and must not put invented words in their mouths (use the sourced quotes below).

## 2. The four honorees

### Ada Yonath (עדה יונת), 1939–2026
| # | Impact fact | Tag | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mapped the **ribosome**, the cell's protein factory. 2009 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, shared with Steitz and Ramakrishnan | V | https://www.jns.org/feature/ada-yonath-the-israeli-chemist-who-dared-to-dream |
| 2 | **About 25,000 attempts** before the first ribosome crystals in 1980, then two more decades to reach full 3D structures (2000–01). Leading scientists thought it was nearly impossible | V | same |
| 3 | Introduced **cryo bio-crystallography** (flash-freezing crystals), now "routine in structural biology". Snap-freezing first destroyed the ribosomes, so she coated the crystals in oil before freezing | V (cryo) / P (oil detail: one Nobel-site snippet, page returned 403) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ada_Yonath ; https://www.nobelprize.org/stories/women-who-changed-science/ada-yonath/ |
| 4 | Showed how **20+ antibiotics** bind to "pockets" in the bacterial ribosome, opening the way to new drugs and to understanding **resistance** | V | Wikipedia (above) ; https://www.calcalist.co.il/article/rjturqqozl |
| 5 | First Israeli woman Nobel laureate, and the first woman to win the Chemistry prize in 45 years. Grew up poor in Jerusalem and was working before age 12 | V | JNS ; Wikipedia |
- **Quote (verbatim):** "People called me a dreamer." Also: "It was my dream to contribute something to humanity." (2009 Weizmann article, quoted in JNS above) [V]. Another line she said: she was called "a dreamer, a fantasist, even the village idiot", and she didn't care [P: Nobel page, seen only as a search snippet].
- **Mechanic seed:** persistence through many tries: grow or freeze a crystal, fail, adjust, retry. A visible counter ("attempt 1… of 25,000") and no punishment for failing. The payoff is fitting an antibiotic into the right "pocket".

### Yayoi Kusama (יאיוי קוסאמה), 1929–2026
| # | Impact fact | Tag | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Polka dots** as a lifelong language from the 1950s on, plus repeating "Infinity Net" paintings | V | https://www.tate.org.uk/tate-etc/issue-51-spring-2021/yayoi-kusama-infinity-mirror-rooms-from-here-to-infinity |
| 2 | **Infinity Mirror Rooms**: the first was *Phalli's Field*, 1965, New York (mirrors make the dots endless). Tate: her rooms show was its "most visited show of all time" | V | same ; https://www.tate.org.uk/press/press-releases/yayoi-kusama-1929-2026 |
| 3 | **The Obliteration Room** (2002–present): a white room that visitors, especially children, cover with colored dot stickers until the white disappears. It began as a children's project at Queensland Art Gallery | V | https://www.qagoma.qld.gov.au/stories/yayoi-kusama-the-obliteration-room |
| 4 | Childhood hallucinations: a flower pattern spread over the walls and her own body ("I felt as if I had begun to self-obliterate"). In **1977** she voluntarily entered a Tokyo psychiatric hospital, lived there, and worked daily at a nearby studio | V | https://www.theartstory.org/artist/kusama-yayoi/ ; https://psychnews.psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.pn.2017.9a21 (search snippet) |
| 5 | **Pumpkins**: her family ran a seed nursery, and as a child she felt a pumpkin "began speaking to me". She called it an alter ego (the yellow dotted pumpkin on Naoshima, 1994) | V | theartstory (above) |
- **Quote (game-safe, verbatim but disputed source):** "Polka dots are a way to infinity." Traced to her book *Manhattan Suicide Addict* (1984), and sometimes dated to a 1968 text [P] (https://www.kunc.org/2017-03-01/priestess-of-polka-dots-yayoi-kusama-gives-gallerygoers-a-taste-of-infinity). Hebrew as Maariv printed it: "נקודות הן הדרך לאינסוף".
- **Sensitive:** "If it were not for art, I would have killed myself a long time ago." is widely attributed, with no primary source found [P]. It **mentions suicide**: keep it out of game dialogue and frame the idea as "art helped her live with her illness".
- **Mechanic seed:** cover a space with dots (Obliteration Room). Players stick dots until the white room "dissolves" into infinity. Mirrors multiply each dot. This is shared, not competitive (it echoes "no spectators").
